# 🐻🌿 Sweet Bear Boba — Website Mock-up

A cozy, single-page website mock-up for **Sweet Bear Boba**, a family-owned
boba café & bakery in Allen, Texas (off Exchange Parkway) known for its
adorable baked-and-filled bears.

## Design notes
- **Faux-ivy accent wall** rendered live in Canvas — trailing across the dark
  botanical hero, the "Ivy Wall" feature card, and the Visit section, with a
  gentle leaf-sway (disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Cozy palette** anchored on deep ivy green with warm tea-milk cream,
  brown-sugar caramel, and bear-blush accents.
- **The family's story** — Korean-heritage husband-and-wife team; he grew up
  in Allen and went through Allen ISD.
- Two-part menu: **bear milk teas** and **baked & filled bears**.
- Full **light/dark theme** with a toggle; responsive; keyboard-accessible.

## Viewing
Open `index.html` in any browser — it's fully self-contained (no build step,
no external assets or fonts).

> All names, prices, phone, email, and address details are placeholders for
> this mock-up.
