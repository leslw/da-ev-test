Our Story

Two hands, one oven, and a hometown.

Sweet Bear Boba is run by a husband-and-wife team who fold their Korean roots into everything on the counter — from the injeolmi dusting on a warm bun to the yuja we steep for the honey citron tea.

He grew up right here in Allen and went through Allen ISD — so opening the doors off Exchange felt a little like coming home.

Every bear is shaped, baked, and filled by hand, in small batches, all day long. Pull up a chair under the ivy, and stay a while.
